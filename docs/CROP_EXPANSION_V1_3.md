# v1.3 Eight new food crop lines (56 -> 64)

New crops: cauliflower, bok choy, shallot, ginger, turmeric, black bean,
quinoa and artichoke. All registered with plantable seed items, crop loot,
eight age stages, full age/pollination model variants, starter seed crafting
recipes, bilingual labels, creative-tab access and biome climate grouping.
Bok choy additionally supports repeat leaf harvesting by right-click.

Crop classification and source links:
https://extension.umn.edu/vegetables
https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/specialty-crops/pollination/requirements

Caveats: Ginger/turmeric are rhizome crops, artichokes are perennials, and
cauliflower heads/vegetative leaves are harvested before flower seed set.
Seed-item crafting and rapid annual growth are game simplifications; texture
files currently reuse vanilla art assets until botanical renders are made.
