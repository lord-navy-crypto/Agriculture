# v1.0 Crop diversity: 44 to 56 crops

New crops: parsnip, arugula, Swiss chard, okra, fennel, dill, cilantro,
parsley, collard greens, green beans, chili peppers and Brussels sprouts.

Each has a native farmland crop block, seven-step age (0..7), seed and food
items, normal maturity-gated drop tables, a distinctive **temporary vanilla item
texture**, bilingual language keys, two-ingredient game crafting recipe and
explicit climate/harvest metadata. Previous 44 crop IDs and their saves remain.

The leaf herbs **arugula, Swiss chard, parsley, collard greens, dill and
cilantro** now support the same repeat right-click harvest as basil/mint:
one leaf item, mature crop returns to age 4 and regrows. Breaking the crop still
obeys vanilla-style loot tables. Seed craft pairings are **gameplay recipes,
not claims about real seed propagation**. These crops still share Minecraft
age models as placeholders (true botanical structures remain an art task).

**Climate:** cool-season plants include parsnip, leafy greens, herbs, fennel,
Brussels sprouts; warm-season plants include okra, snap bean, hot pepper.
The existing biome bands remain game climate proxies, not thermometers.
